The Financial Reality of Higher Education

College is often described as an investment in the future, but how strong is the return after graduation?

Many students pursue higher education to secure stable careers or deepen knowledge in areas they care about. But in the U.S., continuing education has become exceptionally expensive due to rising tuition costs. Many students rely on loans that must be repaid once graduates begin earning — often leaving them with significant student debt.

Using national institutional data, this interactive article explores how student debt and post-enrollment earnings vary across states and types of colleges, offering a clearer perspective on the financial realities of higher education.
